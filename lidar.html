Waypoint 03 · Trail log

LiDAR obstacle-avoidance robot

A robot that used LiDAR to find its way through a maze without hitting the walls. I designed and soldered the circuit board, 3D printed the chassis and wrote the firmware.

RoleTeam of 5
WhenJune 2023
TerrainRobotics · Embedded
KitSoldering · CAD · C
Elevation gained

A lot broke before it worked.

Almost every part failed at least once, so seeing it finally make it through the maze was my favourite moment of the project.

Camp 1 

Getting through a maze we couldn't see

We weren't allowed to see the maze beforehand, so our robot had to work it out on its own. We used a LiDAR sensor to map the walls as it drove.

Camp 2 

Circuit design

Our circuit board didn't work the first time. After a lot of troubleshooting and re-soldering a few joints, it finally did.

Camp 3 

3D printed chassis

I designed a two-layer chassis in Fusion 360, with the LiDAR on top and the circuit board on the layer below.

Camp 4

Writing the firmware

Summit

We made it through the maze!
